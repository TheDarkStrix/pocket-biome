**Pocket Biome — delivery from the three director passes, 2026-11-05**

Current evidence: [Three audit passes and scorecard](DIRECTOR_ITERATIONS.md) · [Source-bound validation](DIRECTOR_VALIDATION.json).

The game now centers its ending on a currently thriving, connected sanctuary. Discovery collection remains a twenty-entry optional mastery activity. Four expedition islands teach environmental rules, reward useful home capabilities, and support eight authored mastery challenges with independent personal bests.

**What the player experiences**

Start with a thirsty Oak, compare Water with the actual **Info** button, observe live growth, gather materials, craft/place a Workbench, and welcome Clover to the first spring. Restore a garden, a working woodland workshop and a climate shelter. Use care tools and spatial arrangements to connect the habitats. Complete the five chapters with a live sanctuary of at least 25 cells; pursue the rest of the Journal, expedition challenges, visitor requests and decoration at your own pace.

New islands use **Living climate**. Conditions approach a local balance shaped by natural neighbors and earned care. Removing a source permits recovery. Existing islands retain their saved growth rules; **Settings → Climate growth rules** allows a deliberate switch while retaining progress, land and production schedules. The inspector explains the current rules and trends. Discoveries stay permanent; a player can cultivate another currently qualifying form, including a same-tier form, without resetting resource cooldowns. Cultivation remains deliberate until natural discovery is resumed.

Select land to reveal **Inspect / Tend / Move / Goal** directly on the island. The compact goal view identifies the current missing relationship. Movement, draft inspection and blueprint placement explain affected neighbors and present habitat gains/losses. Blueprint placement confirms before spending its finished blueprint and rolls back the entire transaction on save failure. Optional moisture, heat and habitat overlays use numbers and geometry as well as colour.

Restoration celebrates the actual island and explains the capability earned. Clover, Sola, Juniper and shelter visitors move through real suitable habitat cells. Their routes respond to changed layouts; field notes preserve the story of their visits. Reduced motion freezes cosmetic movement. Original restoration/visitor feedback sounds are generated by `tools/make_director_audio.py`; waveform checks cover duration and clipping, with human listening acceptance still separate.

Sanctuaries can be named and decorated. Pebble paths, signs, reeds, benches, garden lights, flower rings and firefly lanterns occupy a separate visual layer, cost no habitat cells and change no production or condition statistics. New islands retain an actual starting-land portrait for comparison with the current island. Older saves explicitly say when no starting portrait was recorded. Photo mode remains the route to an exportable image.

Three optional visitor requests encourage different habitat designs without timers, streaks or mandatory chores. Their cosmetic rewards are once-only. Returning players receive a short summary with a direct continuation, their unfinished task and a ready blueprint when available; saved drafts keep priority. There is no fabricated offline production or punishment for a late return.

**Expedition rewards and mastery**

| First restoration | Earned home capability |
|---|---|
| Dry Springs | Water channels can reach through three natural-land steps |
| Frost Hollow | A second shade slot after the Shade canopy is earned |
| Ember Shore | A second warmth slot after the Warmth lantern is earned |
| River Link | Firefly lantern decoration |

Every island offers a season challenge and a careful-placement challenge. The season target is four or fewer turns; longer successful practice restorations remain valid without receiving that mastery result. Careful placement uses empty Meadow only, with a confirmed fresh attempt always available. Each original restoration, relaxed replay, challenge attempt and personal best is saved independently. Faster/fewer-placement personal bests survive worse later attempts. Replays grant no repeat home capability or completion ad milestone. Both challenge types have verified solutions on all four islands.

**Safety, compatibility and proof boundaries**

All new save records are optional and bounded: climate rules, deliberate cultivation, sanctuary identity/portraits/decorations, visitor requests, expedition trials/bests and local playtest preference. Existing completed chapters, discoveries, receipts and entitlements remain valid. Dismantling still supplies recovery at the land cap. The third audit added a regression for fusing cultivated land: the single-tile cultivation lock is cleared and care markers merge onto the sanctuary without multiplying effects or invalidating the save.

Local playtest reporting is off by default, never uploads, excludes entered sanctuary names, and separates island timelines. Its retained 200-event window cannot establish retention. Native operational ad counters distinguish requests from actual SDK callbacks, deduplicate repeated callback events and keep reported currencies separate. They do not establish publisher payout or live revenue. Debug builds still use test advertising/products, current caps and development signing.

The validation report records the exact tested source, campaigns, opening routes, rendered touch walkthrough, package checks and APK checksum. The four campaign probes are accelerated known-solution transactions. The twelve opening explorers follow visible guidance through model authorities. The rendered opening activates actual controls with screen-touch events and uses the live game clock without injected materials or progression. None of these is an unfamiliar-person study, physical-device performance measurement or proof of enjoyment.

**Requirements left for release**

Production publisher/ad and billing setup, intended audience/consent configuration, hosted privacy/support URLs, secure release signing, representative physical Android testing, native provider sandbox flows and private unfamiliar-player observation. These need the corresponding accounts, device access or people; development was not paused to request them. No public application release was performed.
