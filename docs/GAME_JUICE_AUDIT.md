The previous pass left Golem/workbench animation, richer growth, animal behaviour and milestone presentation unfinished. This pass implements those gaps over the retained painted art. The [current inventory](ANIMATION_STATUS.md) states exactly what moves. No replacement 3D world or new game rewards were introduced.

Golem head and arms, Workbench tools/blueprint/lantern and Totem feathers now move through bounded continuous UV rigs. Growth unfurls the canopy above a fixed trunk seam with a local leaf burst. Frogs hop/blink/splash; butterflies flap layered wings; birds perch and fly between actual habitat stops. Local harvests and Pulse affect nearby visitor reactions. Chapter, habitat and finale sheets add a finite leaf/wreath celebration. Photo captures render settled land and omit transient feedback. Reduced motion is static. Paused structures retain their own animation time. Backgrounding/fullscreen ads freeze cosmetic clocks, stop short sounds and suspend ambience; simulation/reward ownership stays in the existing game systems. Milestone controls remain available immediately and retain the existing saved-result ad callbacks. Google test IDs and sandbox services remain selected as requested.
