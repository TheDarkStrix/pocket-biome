# A clear journey from first harvest to thriving sanctuary

The first-session walkthrough still teaches harvest → Water neighbor → discovery/inspection → Workbench → returning spring. After that, tapping the goal opens a live chapter checklist. Every requirement explains its purpose, shows current/required progress, and provides a relevant next action. A saved land hand retains its offers and priority; guidance never spends materials or advances clocks on the player's behalf.

## Advanced lessons

**Goal → Learn** offers a relevant unlocked lesson. **Pack → Learn & revisit** keeps all four lessons available. Skipping is saved and removes the automatic recommendation; the lesson can be started again. Completed lessons retain their full instructions without adding another reward, changing the active lesson or replaying completion progress.

| Lesson | Unlock | Progress follows |
|---|---|---|
| Carry a living river | Restore the first spring | Approach, actual spring, valid grove path, sufficient delivered flow, healthy grove, flower route/flow/growth, seedbed and genuine bloom |
| Shape a sanctuary | Reach chapter three | Shape a matching 2×2 group, review the ready footprint and perform actual fusion |
| Let your island help | Place a Workbench | Find materials, craft/place the Windmill, enable it and earn three Coins through ordinary production |
| Connect a thriving home | Restore the chapter-four climate refuge | Repair currently unsuitable habitats, connect their living route and claim the suitable sanctuary network |

The HUD follows the selected lesson's current step. Tapping it returns to that step. The lesson's primary button opens the actual tool; actions remain subject to the normal preview, confirmation, costs and save checks. Step changes receive short feedback. Completion receipts are optional bounded save data and grant no new currency. Old saves need no migration or reset.

## Objectives and pacing

The checklist covers every requirement in all five chapters, including discoveries, land, Pulse, River, fusion, brick crafting, working structures, automated income and the currently thriving connected ending. Completed rows are marked explicitly. The final ending remains durable if the player later redesigns the island; it does not force exhaustive discovery collection.

Recipe guidance accounts for prerequisites, saved drafts, existing blueprints, running jobs, project capacity and exact material deficits. A missing material leads to a real producing plot with its current ready time. A finished blueprint leads to placement rather than another purchase. Guided recipe links select the right Build category and reset quantity to one so the displayed advice matches the quoted cost. Build's **Help** button exposes the same route. Gather shows the next resource timer when none is ready.

Opening help never fast-forwards production or grants free resources. Project help explicitly suggests gathering or planning neighbors while ordinary crafting runs. Costs and cooldowns were retained: the audit did not supply human evidence that an economy rebalance would improve enjoyment. Campaign pacing reports distinguish individual node collections from actual touch actions; Gather ready can combine many nodes into one action.

## Ad behavior

Existing non-rewarded interstitials remain enabled at eligible natural breaks: at least eight active minutes first, ten minutes between dispatches, at most two per app session, with remove-ads respected. Restored historical chapter rewards are baselined at session start. Opening/advanced lessons, active weather, a saved land hand, a pending rewarded request and fullscreen presentation block dispatch centrally.

No-fill/load failure leaves core play usable. A quiet active-play retry is limited to once per minute and the native bridge independently prevents duplicate loads and preserves consent checks. No retry automatically presents an ad. An already accepted, earned rewarded callback remains payable if a weather event starts while the ad is showing; durable receipts and failed-save rollback still prevent duplicate payouts.

Providers remain test/sandbox integrations. Production IDs, publisher accounts, intended audience/consent setup, privacy/support URLs, signing and real provider/device acceptance remain external release requirements.
