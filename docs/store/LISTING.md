**Name:** Pocket Biome: Tiny Expansion

**Short description (78 characters):**

Grow a floating island. Shape nature, discover biomes, and build a tiny world.

**Full description:**

A tiny island. A world of possibilities.

Start with four floating plots and grow a living landscape above the clouds. Collect natural resources, choose new land from a hand of three cards, and discover what happens when different environments meet.

Water nourishes nearby plants. Forests enrich the ground. Warmth, frost and magic can unlock unexpected transformations. Every placement gives you something new to try.

DISCOVER LIVING BIOMES

Experiment with environmental combinations and uncover 20 discoveries. Your Field Journal keeps each discovery, with free clues that become clearer as you play.

CREATE GREAT SANCTUARIES

Bring four compatible plots together into a sanctuary. Build an Ancient Grove, reveal a Moonlit Lagoon, and explore other combinations as your island grows.

BUILD YOUR OWN LITTLE WORLD

Craft useful structures, keep projects moving, and place finished blueprints on your land. Add a Windmill, Rain Totem or Stone Golem, then upgrade or pause their work when your plans change.

SHAPE NATURE WITH WORLD PULSE

Earn energy through discoveries and objectives. Preview Rain, Heat or Frost over your island, then choose when and where to use it. Canceling a preview spends nothing.

MAKE A WORLD WORTH KEEPING

Follow five progression chapters or pursue your own discoveries. Pan, zoom and turn the island, then frame a photo and choose whether to share it through your device.

PLAY AT YOUR PACE

Core island-building works offline. Progress saves on your device. Optional rewarded ads and cosmetic purchases support the game; no purchase or advertisement is needed to complete its discoveries.

Adjust text size, sound, music, vibration and motion in Settings.

Your next discovery might be one plot away.
